A LITTLE LETTER

For the girl
who leaves me speechless.

I don't really know how to explain what happens to me when I look at you.

Sometimes I have so many things I want to say...

and then I see you.

And suddenly, nothing comes out.

My mind just goes blank.

I don't know if it's your smile, your eyes, the way you look at me, or just the fact that somehow, you can still make me stop for a moment.

You are beautiful.

But honestly, it's not just about how beautiful you look.

It's the little things too.

The way you smile. The way you talk. The random moments. The silly ones.

The moments where we're doing absolutely nothing...

and somehow I still don't want the moment to end.

I wish I could explain exactly what I feel when I'm with you.

But maybe I don't need to.

Maybe some feelings are just too big for words.

So I'll just say the simple thing.

I love looking at you.

I love having you in my life.

And I love that, somehow, you can still make me completely speechless.

So if you ever wonder what goes through my mind when I look at you...

now you know.

Nothing.

Because I'm too busy thinking about how beautiful you are.
